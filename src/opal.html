Opal

Making focus feel a little easier. Opal already blocks distractions. With two teammates, I designed an expansion of the app around planning, motivation, and breaks worth taking, built from a 30-student survey and tested with 23 more.

Role
Product Designer
Team
3 designers
Timeline
10 weeks, 2025
Project
Expansion of the Opal app
Recognition
Indigo Design Award

The opportunity

Students didn’t want stricter blocking.

We surveyed 30 college students. They weren’t asking for stricter blocking. They wanted help staying motivated, planning their time, and taking breaks without falling straight back into scrolling.

27/30
struggled with procrastination
21/30
were distracted by their phones
61%
had never used a focus app

What we found

Focus apps were each solving one piece of the problem.

  • ForestBlocks distractions.
  • FinchMakes self-care rewarding.
  • Focus FriendAdds accountability.

Students wanted pieces of all three. Opal already had the blocking, so we designed the rest.

What we made

Three new features for the whole cycle: plan, focus, step away.

What I learned

Make the better choice the easier one.

The real question wasn’t how to stop distraction. It was how to make putting the phone down feel better than picking it back up.
