Opal · Product Design

Making focus feel a little easier

Students don’t need another app telling them to focus. With two teammates, I designed a focus app around planning, motivation, and breaks worth taking, built from a 30-student survey and tested with 23 more.

Role
Product Designer
Team
Brayden, Chris DeMassa, Bess Overton
Timeline
10 weeks, 2025
Recognition
Indigo Design Award
OverviewInsightDesignTestingTakeaway

Overview

Students know their phones are distracting. Doing something about it is the hard part.

We surveyed 30 college students. They weren’t asking for stricter blocking. They wanted help staying motivated, planning their time, and taking breaks without falling straight back into scrolling.

27/30
struggled with procrastination
21/30
were distracted by their phones
61%
had never used a focus app

Insight

Focus apps were each solving one piece of the problem.

  • ForestBlocks distractions.
  • FinchMakes self-care rewarding.
  • Focus FriendAdds accountability.

Students wanted pieces of all three. That became the foundation for Opal.

Design

One app for the whole cycle: plan, focus, step away.

Testing

23 people showed us where it still wasn’t clear.

  • “Event” didn’t make senseWe switched to familiar words like Task and Activity, with icons.
  • Scanning needed guidanceWe added alignment guides and clearer onboarding for Tap Stickers.
  • New users got lostWe added help the first time someone meets a feature.

What I took away

A focus app shouldn’t run on willpower. It should make the better choice easier.

The real question wasn’t how to stop distraction. It was how to make putting the phone down feel better than picking it back up.
