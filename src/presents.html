Presents

Capture a moment, together. A private time capsule for a small group of friends. Add the little things as they happen, then open them all together. With Chris and Finley, I designed it for the SVSD Design Sprint and built the iOS app, the widget and a playable demo at bepresents.org.

Goal
Help a small group of friends hold onto the little moments of senior year, not just the big nights.
The idea
If the app picks the format and seals every moment until the group opens it together, friends will capture the small stuff they’d usually skip, because there’s nothing to perfect and a reveal night to look forward to.
How I’d know
Moments added per person each week, and whether groups actually come back for reveal night. If people stopped adding after the first few days, I’d be wrong.

Goal

Presents is for the times we don’t capture.

We’re seniors in college, and our time together is almost up. We want to remember more than the big nights. We want the little moments in between. We call them presents.

The idea

The app picks. Nobody peeks. Everyone opens it together.

  • The app picksShuffle for a photo, video, voice note, doodle or a few words. One take.
  • Sealed, even from youOnce it’s in, nobody sees it. Not even you.
  • Reveal nightPut it on the TV. It opens section by section, and everything from one moment shows up together.
  • Start in personHold your phones close to make the group. No accounts.
  • Hide anythingAnyone can quietly hide a moment. Nobody sees who.

The idea, built

A real app, not just screens.

I built Presents in SwiftUI: a home screen widget and a lock screen control that open straight into the shuffle, groups and sealed moments shared across phones, and a reveal night that runs phone to phone with no server, as long as everyone’s in the same room.

What changed my mind

What we cut and loosened along the way.

  • The “document this?” nudgeThe app used to notice when friends were together and suggest a moment. We cut it.
  • No choosing, mostlyThe shuffle still picks the format, but you get one free reroll.
  • Sign-up became a noteAfter a full run-through of the site, we swapped the email sign-up for a leave-us-a-note box.
