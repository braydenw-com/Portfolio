Presents

Capture a moment, together. A private time capsule for a small group of friends. Made with Chris and Finley for the SVSD Design Sprint.

Role
Designer & iOS build
Team
3 designers
Built
iOS app, widget, demo site
For
SVSD Design Sprint
Goal
Hold onto the little moments of senior year, not just the big nights.
The idea
If the app picks the format and seals everything until the group opens it together, friends will capture what they’d usually skip.
How I’d know
Moments added per person each week. If people stop after the first few days, I’m wrong.
What changed my mind
We cut the “document this?” nudge, and “no choosing” became one free reroll.

The idea, built

The app picks. Nobody peeks.

Reveal night

Put it on the TV. Open it together.
