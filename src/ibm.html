Design at enterprise speed

Twelve weeks on IBM’s Core Software Data Design team in Austin. Most of it went to how people and AI agents learn complex products in Db2. Then came a side mission that ended up live on IBM.com.

Role

Goal
Make IBM’s support offerings clear enough that customers understand them and take the next step.
The idea
If each page leads with what the offering does for the customer and a clear next step, more visitors will sign up or reach out, because the old pages buried the value.

IBM Db2 · Product guidance

How does a product teach both people and agents?

Most of my summer went to product guidance in Db2: research, exploration, prototyping, and a lot of playbacks with the team. It shaped how I think about AI, agents, and the future of product guidance.

The idea · IBM Support pages

Ask on Thursday. Presenting on Friday.

Near the end of the internship, my manager asked if anyone had time to redesign a IBM Support pages that weren’t showing their offerings well. I said yes. The bet was that leading with what each offering does for the customer, with one clear next step, would fix that. I took them from Figma to code with IBM Bob and worked inside Drupal’s constraints.
