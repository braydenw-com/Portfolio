IBM Db2 · Product guidance

How does a product teach both people and agents?

Most of my summer went to product guidance in Db2: research, exploration, prototyping, and a lot of playbacks with the team. It shaped how I think about AI, agents, and the future of product guidance.

IBM Support · Design and build

Ask on Thursday. Presenting on Friday.

Near the end of the internship, my manager asked if anyone had time to redesign three IBM Support pages that weren’t showing their offerings well. I said yes, took them from Figma to code with IBM Bob, and worked inside Drupal’s constraints.

What I took away

If products are learning to work with us, how should we design the way they learn?

I left more comfortable working inside complexity, sharing unfinished work, and knowing when to keep exploring versus when to make something real. And more likely to raise my hand when something interesting comes up.
