Things I believe.

  1. Good designers should have a life outside design.

    The things I do away from a screen usually make me better when I come back to one.

  2. Going into the field beats another hour in Figma.

    Seeing how people actually work, talk, improvise, and solve problems tells me more than assumptions ever could.

  3. The best networking usually doesn’t feel like networking.

    Most of the relationships that have mattered started with curiosity, not asking someone for something.

  4. Complex products don’t need to feel complex.

    Some of my favorite design problems are the ones where the system underneath is incredibly complicated, but the experience doesn’t have to be.

  5. I’ll probably say yes to the weird project.

    Those tend to be the ones I learn the most from.
