A little more about me.

I’m Brayden, a UX design senior at SCAD in Savannah, graduating March 2027.

I’m a curious designer who likes documenting the process out loud, focused on human-centered design and where AI fits into it — lately I’ve been especially interested in how people stay in control of the AI agents they use.

Previously, I interned at IBM in Austin on the Db2 team, where my first design shipped in July, and worked on SCADpro projects with Deloitte and Hartford HealthCare. I’ve also held campus roles with Framer and Adobe.

Right now I’m Director of Partnerships at SCAD FLUX, plus two other SCAD roles in admissions and career & alumni success. My senior project is an ag-tech solution helping cattle ranchers deal with New World Screwworm — and most Sundays, I post a list of open design roles on LinkedIn.

I like making complicated things feel a little more understandable, useful, and human. Outside of all that: pottery, coffee shops, and asking a lot of questions.

Things I believe.

Good designers should have a life outside design.

The things I do away from a screen usually make me better when I come back to one.

Going into the field beats another hour in Figma.

Seeing how people actually work, talk, improvise, and solve problems tells me more than assumptions ever could.

The best networking usually doesn’t feel like networking.

Most of the relationships that have mattered started with curiosity, not asking someone for something.

Complex products don’t need to feel complex.

Some of my favorite design problems are the ones where the system underneath is incredibly complicated, but the experience doesn’t have to be.

I’ll probably say yes to the weird project.

Those tend to be the ones I learn the most from.

But design is only part of it.

going somewhere new, making things away from a screen, finding really good food, and spending time with good people.

Anyway, that’s me.

There’s more to the story, but it’s probably better over coffee.
