A little more about me.

I’m Brayden, a product designer studying UX at SCAD.

I’m interested in the space where people, emerging technology, and complicated systems meet.

Previously, I designed at IBM, working on products and guidance for technical and AI-driven experiences. Outside of product work, I help build design communities with Adobe and Framer, organize events, make ceramics, and occasionally end up researching cows.

I like making complicated things feel a little more understandable, useful, and human.

Things I believe.

Good designers should have a life outside design.

The things I do away from a screen usually make me better when I come back to one.

Going into the field beats another hour in Figma.

Seeing how people actually work, talk, improvise, and solve problems tells me more than assumptions ever could.

The best networking usually doesn’t feel like networking.

Most of the relationships that have mattered started with curiosity, not asking someone for something.

Complex products don’t need to feel complex.

Some of my favorite design problems are the ones where the system underneath is incredibly complicated, but the experience doesn’t have to be.

I’ll probably say yes to the weird project.

Those tend to be the ones I learn the most from.

But design is only part of it.

going somewhere new, making things away from a screen, finding really good food, and spending time with good people.

Anyway, that’s me.

There’s more to the story, but it’s probably better over coffee.
