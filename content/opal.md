## opportunity label
The opportunity

## opportunity heading
Students didn’t want stricter blocking.

## opportunity paragraph
We surveyed 30 college students. They weren’t asking for stricter blocking. They wanted help staying motivated, planning their time, and taking breaks without falling straight back into scrolling.

## decision label
The decision

## decision heading
Build on Opal instead of starting a new app.

## decision paragraph
Students liked pieces of Forest, Finch, and Focus Friend, but no single app did it all. Rather than design one more focus app, we added planning, rewards, and better breaks to Opal, which already handled the blocking. It meant working inside an existing product instead of starting fresh, and it meant students wouldn’t need another app.

## made label
What we made

## made heading
Three new features for the whole cycle: plan, focus, step away.

## made caption 1
**Calendar**Classes, tasks, and to-dos live in one plan inside Opal, so deciding what to do and starting it happen in the same place.

## made caption 2
**Tap Stickers**Collectible gem stickers you place around your space. Tap your phone on one and Opal starts that spot’s block, like Home.

## made caption 3
**Break Suggestions**When you reach for a blocked app, Opal’s pause screen suggests something better to do: step outside, read, dance.
