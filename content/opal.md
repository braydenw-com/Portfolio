## made label
What we made

## made heading
Three new features for the whole cycle: plan, focus, step away.

## made caption 1
**Calendar**Classes, tasks, and to-dos live in one plan inside Opal, so deciding what to do and starting it happen in the same place.

## made caption 2
**Tap Stickers**Collectible gem stickers you place around your space. Tap your phone on one and Opal starts that spot’s block, like Home.

## made caption 3
**Break Suggestions**When you reach for a blocked app, Opal’s pause screen suggests something better to do: step outside, read, dance.
