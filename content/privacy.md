## intro summary
**Short version:** this site counts visits with Google Analytics. I don’t collect your name or email, and I don’t sell anything.

## whats collected label
What’s collected

## whats collected heading
Just enough to see what people look at.

## whats collected paragraph 1
Google Analytics records things like which pages you visit, how long you stay, roughly where you are (country or city), and what device and browser you use. It sets cookies to tell a new visit from a returning one. I use this only to see which projects people look at.

## whats collected paragraph 2
Google processes this data under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). There are no ads on this site, and the data isn’t used for advertising.
